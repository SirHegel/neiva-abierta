# Texturas de fachada

Se generaron dos módulos de fachada con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`,
el 7 de septiembre de 2026 UTC, para sustituir el patrón de ventanas dibujado
en canvas por superficies de apariencia fotográfica. Son recreaciones visuales,
no fotografías ni reconstrucciones verificadas de edificios concretos de Neiva.

Archivos conservados en el proyecto:

- `public/facades/neiva-residential.png`: fachada residencial/comercial de dos pisos.
- `public/facades/neiva-upper.png`: módulo repetible de pisos superiores.

Prompts completos:

## Fachada residencial

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless architectural facade texture for a realistic 3D city game set in Neiva, Huila, Colombia. Create a single wide 3072x1536 pixel photographic texture atlas, completely filled by an orthographic, flat straight-on view of a continuous two-story Colombian urban residential/commercial facade, with no perspective convergence. This must look like an actual high resolution photograph of real construction, not a render, illustration, game or low-poly art. Three neighboring narrow building bays across the image; pale warm off-white stucco first bay, weathered cream stucco middle, softly faded terracotta/beige third bay. Upper story has realistic dark aluminum-frame windows with physically believable soft glass reflections, worn concrete sills, slim metal security bars on some windows, one modest shallow metal balcony. Ground story includes a dark steel garage door, a pedestrian wood/metal door, one modest closed roller-shutter storefront, small tiled plinth. Colombian hot-climate vernacular with subtle grime, hairline cracks, faded plaster, fine microtexture. Consistent two floors both 3 meters tall, together filling entire image exactly; no sky, pavement, vegetation, people, vehicles, signs, lettering, logos, watermarks, border, margins, or roofs. Uniform neutral cloudy-day illumination, no directional shadows or highlights; intended as diffuse albedo texture. Edges join seamlessly horizontally with plain stucco at both edges. Three bays are one continuous photographic elevation, no panel borders. Strongly prioritize photographic realism and ordinary lived-in Neiva-area architecture; it is fictional architecture, not a claimed specific real building.

## Pisos superiores

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless wall elevation texture for a realistic 3D Neiva Colombia city simulation. Create a 2048x2048 photorealistic albedo photograph, ortho straight-on entire image is facade, absolutely no perspective. A continuous facade of an ordinary Colombian mid-rise urban apartment/office building: two identical-height floors, three evenly spaced window bays across. Weathered warm ivory painted concrete, subtle warm grey structural vertical columns, realistic recessed dark aluminum-framed sliding windows, softly reflected overcast pale sky in glass, slender concrete external sun shades above the windows, slight weather staining below sills, detailed imperfections fine gritty plaster. Neutral diffuse overcast illumination, no strong sun or cast shadows, no people, plants, sky, ground, roofs, text, signage, borders or margins. Both vertical and horizontal image edges must tile seamlessly, with plaster edges and floor-slab pattern repeat. No doors, no shops or pavement: upper floors ONLY so it repeats on a taller building. Real material microstructure and photographic detail, no cartoon, no blocky voxel look, no videogame screenshot. The image is a fictional facade module for a real-time game, not a specific real building.
